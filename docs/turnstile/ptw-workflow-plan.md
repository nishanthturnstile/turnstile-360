# Permit to Work discovery and delivery plan

Decision proposal for Turnstile 360, 19 November 2026. The customer buys one optional PTW module. Within that installation, each site publishes its own operating procedure. A worker moving between sites can recognize the same permit stages and see exactly which local checks apply. The five requested permit types form the initial product scope. A site can activate each independently after qualification.

Read the [industry comparison](ptw-industry-research.md), [policy decisions](ptw-policy-decisions.md) and [module build audit](ptw-build-readiness.md) alongside the [accepted workspace architecture](../../architecture.md). The design below is proposed for planning. Release qualification and site acceptance are future work.

## The workflow people use

| Stage                 | Responsible person and digital work                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Condition for moving on                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prepare               | Requester describes one job, selects a hierarchy location and equipment, names the holder, crew, reviewer and approver, and proposes a shift. Create a stable reference immediately. The site board shows other work in the area.         | Save partial drafts freely. Show each gap beside the affected person, plan or control and who can resolve it.                                                                                                  |
| Assess on site        | Assessor walks the job with the performing team. Record observations, controls, notes, photos, time, position and its accuracy. Link JSA, risk assessment, specialist records and the isolation authority’s evidence.                     | Capturing a position proves only a reading. Completing the assessment requires the assessor’s explicit verification and the controls required by the site.                                                     |
| Coordinate and review | Create a high-priority review task and a dependent approval task due by start. Area authority examines simultaneous operations, shared isolation, adjacent zones and actual conditions. Record conflicting references and decision owner. | Return opens a correction revision. Resubmission carries forward every prior decision. Reject and cancel have separate reasons and outcomes.                                                                   |
| Authorize and issue   | Named authorities decide in sequence against a specific permit revision. Show passed, outstanding, failed and excepted checks with source, time and validator.                                                                            | The final authority issues the permit for its defined scope and window. Each allowed exception preserves the original result, compensating controls, authority and expiry. Revalidation retires the exception. |
| Accept and start      | Holder reads back scope, restrictions, emergency arrangements and validity to the crew. Capture acknowledgement and required field evidence.                                                                                              | Server verifies current authority, clearance, competencies, location and dependencies at start. Approved work awaiting start has a separate board column.                                                      |
| Work and hand over    | Field view shows restrictions, crew, device health and latest accepted readings. At relief, outgoing and incoming responsible persons record plant condition, outstanding controls and acceptance.                                        | A handover cannot extend the permit. A change of crew, scope, location or validity creates a revision and holds affected work until reauthorized.                                                              |
| Stop and recover      | Allow immediate stop reporting. Record suspension or expiry, reason, time, device/event and deliveries. Keep exit and emergency response available. Create investigation work per policy.                                                 | Only an authorized restart on reassessed evidence can resume eligible work. An expired authorization needs fresh validity and authority. Resolving an alert or closing a task cannot restart it.               |
| Hand back and close   | Performing authority accounts for workers, tools, housekeeping and photos. Area and isolation authorities verify clearance and disposition of shared isolations before accepting handback.                                                | Preserve signed closure and release evidence. Expired, cancelled or rejected records retain their distinct disposition. An expired job still needs safe handback if work started.                              |

**Example.** A contractor plans welding on a vessel while another team holds an entry permit for the same vessel. Creation shows both references. The area authority records how the jobs will be sequenced. Even if welding review finished yesterday, starting it today rechecks the entry roster, shared isolation and current clearance. A delayed exit message cannot release an isolation or authorize welding. The area authority can see which check still needs evidence.

## How to configure variation

Use typed, versioned policies with named decisions. Start with jurisdiction and industry templates, overlay installation and site rules, then resolve type, task hazards, asset and shift. Show the effective rule and its origin to the requester. Combine mandatory controls cumulatively. An incompatible pair of rules blocks policy publication until a competent owner resolves it. Treat mixed hazards as multiple applicable controls on one job, with separately approved linked authorizations where required.

Give site administrators a draft editor, human-readable change comparison, examples of affected permits and a simulation against historical or synthetic jobs. Require a second authorized publisher, effective date, scope and reason. Pin the policy and evidence revisions to each decision. A prospective policy change applies to new requests. For an urgent safety change, find affected live work, hold it and obtain reassessment. Rollback publishes another revision. Translate role names, labels, languages and checklist order without changing the meaning of a signed decision.

Keep stable across installations the identities of people, permits and assets, chronological history, tenant and site authority, distinct issue/start, and the requirement for positive evidence of required controls. Give administrators controlled choices for applicability, approval levels, competent roles, duty conflicts, mandatory evidence, validity, tolerances, response deadlines and delivery channels. A custom field may collect extra evidence. Introducing a new safety decision or hardware command requires an engineered and qualified change.

## Evidence people can act on

Use the existing people and structure identities. A PTW personnel register supplements those identities with employment, contractor company, contact, medical fitness status/expiry, first-aid level and verified qualifications. Keep historical assignments after deactivation. Match external course completions by stable worker identifiers with human reconciliation for ambiguities. Restrict medical detail to the appropriate roles. Show the requester eligibility, reason and expiry. Recompute eligibility over the required work interval whenever the crew or validity changes.

Give every linked JSA, assessment, plan, isolation and checklist a source ID, version, evidence status and return link. Permit users can select existing records or start drafts with job, location, activity, department, preparer and review context prefilled. Save source, verifier, attachment/replacement history and evidence used at decision time. If a source is unavailable, show its last verification and age. Permit a verified external record only where published policy accepts it. A draft or an unavailable mandatory source cannot silently pass.

For isolation, record applicability even when exempt. Where required, identify equipment, energy sources, matrix version, points, locks/tags, applying and verifying people, method, result, location evidence and release. Track each permit sharing the isolation. Changing one point triggers reassessment of every affected permit. Closure checks the actual authorized release or site-controlled handback sequence across all dependent work.

### Confined space as a separately approved operation

A supervisor prepares a referenced checklist for a named space and entry operation, recording prior contents, site, contacts, hazards, access, capacity, roster, briefing and rescue arrangements. Capture applicable verified PPE and equipment certificates, including calibration/maintenance status and next due time. A blank gear selection blocks approval. Checklist and permit have independent decisions and versions. Approval of one cannot approve the other.

Where a two-stage site procedure applies, distinguish preparation access from bodily entry. A preparation action cannot authorize entry into a hazardous space. Persist ventilation start, interruption and verified completion against the entry operation. A second device reopening the operation sees the same cycle. Invalidate interrupted cycles. Record atmosphere by sampling point, calibrated device, tester, time, units, oxygen, LEL, H₂S, CO, temperature and notes. Evaluate boundary conditions and freshness under the approved procedure.

An attendant checks the authorized entrant in against the current permit, checklist, capacity, controls and monitoring status. Concurrent admissions compete for the same remaining place. One person can have only one open entry for that operation. Record purpose, entry and exit, calculate time inside and show current occupancy during an outage response. SOS records who, where, when, emergency type, delivery attempts, acknowledgements and resolution. Provide a site-approved fallback for loss of connectivity. Exit and muster remain possible during suspension. Completion requires zero open entries, secured-space decision, actor and notes. Offer draft cancellation, search, status and space-type breakdown, active entrants and recent activity.

## Automate the paperwork and preserve field judgment

Start with deterministic automation. Prefill the crew and location from authoritative records, calculate shift limits, detect overlaps, queue review tasks, route notices and expire eligibility at its recorded time. Show one actionable reason per failed check. For example, “Asha’s gas-tester qualification expires before the proposed end” should link to the credential and let the requester revise the team or window. A successful lookup has no authority to certify conditions at the worksite.

| Optional AI assistance     | What the user receives                                                                                 | Acceptance and fallback                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Extract certificate fields | Candidate asset ID, issuer, dates and page image with the source region highlighted                    | A verifier resolves ambiguity and confirms the match. Missing pages, ambiguous dates, an unknown worker or poor image quality go to manual entry.                   |
| Prepare a request          | Draft job description from dictated notes and suggested plan links from records the user may read      | Requester checks the transcription, scope and links. Site policy determines mandatory controls even when the model omits a hazard.                                  |
| Prepare shift handover     | Summary of changes since last acceptance with links to signed decisions, alarms and unresolved actions | Incoming holder sees the original evidence, data age and all deterministic blockers before acknowledging.                                                           |
| Help the reviewer          | Explain failed checks in plain language or translate the crew briefing                                 | Bind each explanation to the exact policy and evidence version. Preserve approved source text and provide a competent interpreter where the procedure requires one. |

Enable AI per installation, use only authorized records and an approved processing location, and record model version, source versions, edits and accepting person. Treat uploaded content as untrusted input. Keep model output outside the command path for issue, exceptions, gas limits, release, entry and restart. An unavailable model leaves all manual workflows usable. Before rollout, use de-identified cases covering misleading certificates, obsolete policies, mixed languages and omitted hazards. Measure incorrect accepted suggestions, unsupported statements, missed blockers, correction time and time saved. Set site acceptance criteria before the trial and disable any use case that fails. This evaluation approach draws on the [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1).

## Integration and reporting contract

Turnstile API owns commands, policy evaluation and state. The responsive web client and a future mobile client submit the same commands. DMS owns document bytes and versions through authenticated HTTP. Optional peer modules supply records and consume events via public contracts. Keep site operating policy and worker data inside the customer installation. The central control plane selects and delivers product capabilities.

| Consumer                                                | Contract and behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Register, field board, mobile and QR                    | Resolve the stable reference under current viewer scope. Show type, location, holder, first aider, crew, restrictions, validity and blockers. QR contains an opaque reference. Begin with online authorization. Pending offline observations can be reconciled with identity, observed time and server receipt time.                                                                                             |
| Action Tracker, dashboard, management review and alerts | Project review, dependent approval and investigation tasks using permit, revision and task purpose as the deduplication key. Return/reject/cancel superseded tasks. Record retries, delivery outcomes, acknowledgement and escalation separately.                                                                                                                                                                |
| Reports, analytics and custom dashboards                | Count requested jobs by first submission, issued by first final authorization, closed by actual handback/closure date. Count each permit once per defined measure. Record reissues/extensions separately. Active at time T requires start, valid interval and permission to proceed at T. Reconstruct daily snapshots using site time zone. Persist report period, filters, generation time and readable output. |
| Documents, Evidence Audit and CAPA                      | Publish a versioned permit packet on eligible transitions. Check audience at retrieval. Propagate archive/restore under retention and holds. Findings reference immutable evidence revisions. A corrective action uses a unique finding/action key to survive repeated submission.                                                                                                                               |
| Incident, risk and barrier review                       | Supply reference, authority, validity at incident time, controls and recorded violations. A later correction is separately timestamped. Limit worker detail to the investigation audience.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Devices, monitoring, plans and LOTO                     | Validate source, timestamp, units, device, asset and work-zone binding. Track lost signal and late events. Preserve rejected check-ins with reason, uncertainty and actor. A configured dwell time handles sustained drift. A stale safe reading cannot reverse a more recent stop.                                                                                                                              |

Commit the decision and its pending event delivery together. Commands carry an expected permit revision and a retry key. Consumers remember event IDs, detect revision gaps and reconcile with the authoritative record. Re-evaluate live eligibility before acting even when a dashboard or notification is delayed. Enforce audience, assignment and site scope for queries, exports, bulk actions, archive, restore and administrator operations. Retention and backup recovery must preserve the link between permit, evidence and decisions. Exercise retries after intervening changes and two simultaneous starts for incompatible jobs.

## Release sequence and evidence for acceptance

The selected first workflow is Confined Space. The [final implementation plan](ptw-confined-space-implementation-plan.md) owns its delivery sequence, LMS dependency and release gates. Hot Work, Electrical Work, Work at Height and Excavation follow separate qualification after the confined-space pilot.

Before the pilot, collect a blank permit, a completed handback, a rejected request, a shift-change example, a contractor evidence set, simultaneous-work rules and the site's emergency procedure. Ask the site authority to exercise them against the proposed policy editor. Record time spent locating evidence, duplicate data entry, correction loops, expired approvals offered for start, unmatched worker identities, delayed stops and successful emergency accounting. Compare the same job classes before and after rollout. Throughput alone would reward skipping checks.

No new workflow engine, separate PTW service or mobile app is needed for the proposed first increment. The decisions requiring customer input are operational: the first jurisdiction and site, accountable issuer, approved type procedures, source systems, data residency and fallback arrangements. The delivery team can qualify the module boundary before those decisions are final.

## Initial template acceptance checklist

These are the requested product minimums for the five hazard templates. Apply each requirement at its responsible role and stage. An additional hazard can bring in another template's controls.

| Template                  | Conditions the pilot must demonstrate                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hot Work · High           | Hot-work and firefighting qualifications, assigned fire watch, cleared combustibles, available extinguisher, gas and ignition controls, exclusion zone and site-defined post-work watch.                                                 |
| Confined Space · Critical | Qualified entrants, tester and rescue capability, contractor eligibility, JSA and risk assessment, verified isolation and location, first-aid cover, valid equipment certificates, selected gear, approved checklist and entry sequence. |
| Electrical Work · High    | Electrical and LOTO qualifications, isolation and verified absence of voltage, voltage-appropriate PPE and second worker. Energized work follows its own approved procedure if introduced.                                               |
| Work at Height · High     | Approved plan, working-at-height and fall-arrest competency, rescue-trained team member, harness and lanyard certificates, inspected anchors, edge protection, weather and drop-zone checks.                                             |
| Excavation · High         | Trained crew, excavation plan, located utilities, soil classification, protective system, safe access, edge protection, applicable atmosphere testing and assigned spotter.                                                              |

For preparation, suggest an eight-hour window only when the approved type and shift permit it. Require end after start and show any earlier shift or evidence cutoff. Every extension requires a fresh risk assessment and on-site location evidence, a renewed conflict decision, and authorization before continuation.
